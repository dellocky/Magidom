Weaver - Complexity level 5/5
	Identity - Female Dual Shortsword, Close range, diving

	Passive - Dancer
		   Everytime the player controlling weaver casts a spell (global spell or a unit within radius), 
		   Weaver gains becomes charged with said element for a small duration holding up
		   a set number maximum, auto attacks while empowered will inflict the effects
		   of the charges she is empowered by

		   Casting a spell while charged will augment the spell with the most recently gained charge
		   adding that spells elemental effect to the terrain where it is cast
	

		   NOTES - Think of charge as a stack, with the most recent spell going to the front
		   - if another spell is cast while charge is full it will push the least recently gained
		   charge out
